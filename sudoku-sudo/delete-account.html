Sudoku Sudo: delete your account and data

App: Sudoku Sudo · Developer: NY Dev Studio

Delete from the app

  1. Open Sudoku Sudo.
  2. Tap the More tab at the bottom, then Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data at the bottom of the page and confirm.

Your account and the data listed below are deleted immediately and permanently.

If you can't access the app

Email nydevstudio.support@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" and include the username you use in the app (e.g. @player). If you signed in with Google, writing from that Google account's email address helps us confirm the account is yours.

We will complete your request within 30 days and let you know by email.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account (guest or Google sign-in)
  • Your name, username and character
  • Your league points, league and weekly group entry
  • Your friendships and friend requests
  • Your cloud backup (statistics, streak, gold and diamonds, themes, achievements, unfinished games)
  • All game data on your phone

Data that may be kept

  • Completed duel records may still contain your name and the result. You can ask us to delete these at the same email address; we will delete them within 30 days.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics do not include your name or email; they are kept according to Google's retention periods (up to 14 months).

See the privacy policy for details.
