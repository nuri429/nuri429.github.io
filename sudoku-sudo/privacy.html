Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what data the Sudoku Sudo app ("the app") processes and why. The app is developed by NY Dev Studio. Questions and requests: nydevstudio.support@gmail.com

This policy was written with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in mind.

In short

  • We don't ask for your email, phone number or real name to play.
  • For leagues, friends and duels, the name, username and character you choose are visible to other players.
  • If you sign in with Google, your progress is backed up to the cloud so you can continue on another device.
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads.
  • You can delete your account and data from the app at any time.

Account types

Guest account: Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous user ID. It contains no information that identifies you. Your progress is stored only on your phone.

Google account: If you sign in with Google, your Google account's email address, name and account ID are passed to Firebase Authentication. Your email address is never shown to other players. Your game progress (statistics, streak, gold and diamonds, unlocked themes, achievements, unfinished games) is backed up to the cloud. Only you can read this backup.

What other players can see

  • The name, username and character you choose.
  • Your league points, league and weekly group.
  • For players on your friends list: your friend requests and weekly points.
  • During a duel: your name, character and progress on the puzzle; the result once it ends.

Data that stays on your device

  • If you add a profile photo, it stays on your phone and is never sent to a server. Other players see your character, not your photo.
  • Your language, sound, vibration and notification settings stay on your phone.
  • Reminder notifications are scheduled on your phone; no server is used for them.

Camera

If you use "Scan QR" to add a friend, the app asks for camera permission. The image is processed on your phone; no photo or video is saved or sent anywhere. We only ask for this permission when you tap that button.

Invite links

If you install the app from Google Play through a friend's invite link, on first launch the app reads only the inviter's username from Google Play's install referrer and suggests adding them as a friend.

Ads

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location) and device information. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent to personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings.

Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Crash reports and usage statistics

To improve the app we use Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (events such as starting or winning a game and completing the tutorial). This data does not include your name or email address.

Service providers and data transfers

Data is processed by Google services: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob. Google may process this data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. We do not sell your data.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Retention

Your data on our servers is kept until you delete your account. If you sign out of a guest account, that account's server records are deleted. Crash reports and usage statistics follow Google's retention periods.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to More > Profile > "Delete my account and data" to permanently delete your account, your server records (name, username, league points, friendships, cloud backup) and the data on your phone.

If you can't access the app, email nydevstudio.support@gmail.com with your username and we will delete your data within 30 days. Completed duel records may still contain your name and the result; you can ask us to delete those at the same address.

Details: https://nuri429.github.io/sudoku-sudo/delete-account.html

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

Your rights

Under KVKK and GDPR you have the right to access your data, to ask for it to be corrected, deleted or restricted, and to object to its processing. Send your requests to nydevstudio.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and in the app, with the last-updated date at the top.
